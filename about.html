Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Maplewood, North Star Bakery began with a simple mission: to bring traditional sourdough breadmaking back to our local community. What started as a small stall at the weekly farmer's market grew into a full-scale neighborhood bakery driven by passion, early mornings, and a commitment to quality craftsmanship.

Over the years, we have grown from a two-person operation into a close-knit team of bakers, pastry chefs, and community members. Every loaf we bake is shaped by hand and fermented over 48 hours to ensure maximum flavor, digestability, and texture.

Sourcing & Baking Philosophy

We believe that great bread starts with honest ingredients. That is why we partner directly with regional grain mills and local dairy farmers to source organic, unbleached flour, stone-ground whole grains, and fresh European-style butter.

  • 100% Organic Flours: Sourced regionally without synthetic additives or preservatives.
  • Wild Yeast Fermentation: No commercial rapid yeasts; all sourdoughs rely on our continuous natural starter.
  • Zero Food Waste Initiative: Unsold bread at the end of each day is donated directly to local community shelters.
